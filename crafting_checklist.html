🎯 Farming Targets for Earthshatter Crafting Materials

Sell what you don't need/use. Focus on Essences (crafting) + Harvest (forced crafts) + Recombinator bases.

🔮 Essences (Primary Crafting)

  • Essence of Contempt (Flat Phys) - Weapon
  • Essence of Dread (Armour) - Chest
  • Essence of Loathing (Attack Speed) - Gloves/Boots
  • Essence of Zeal (Move Speed + Onslaught) - Boots
  • Essence of Torment (Avoid Shock) - Helmet
  • Essence of Horror (Crit Reduction) - Helmet/Chest

Farm: Essence Monoliths, Memory modifiers, Corrupted Essences. Sell excess T1-T3 essences.

🌾 Harvest (Forced Crafts)

  • Physical Damage Reduction (8% PDR) - Chest
  • Augment Physical (Weapon)
  • Augment Armour (Chest/Helmet)
  • Augment Life (All gear)
  • Augment Speed (Boots/Gloves)
  • Force Specific Mods (Crit Reduction, etc.)

Farm: Wild/Vivid/Primal Roots in maps. Harvest crafts are deterministic - use for forced PDR, Life, Res.

🔬 Recombinator (Weapon Crafting)

  • Vaal Axes (ilvl83+, 27%+ Quality)
  • Elders Exalted Orbs (Elder Influence)
  • Brine Hook (Aspect suffix)
  • Kishara's Decat / Annul Orbs
  • Tempering Orbs (8% Phys)
  • Warlord Exalted Orbs (Slams)

Buy bases in bulk. Recombinator crafting is RNG but profitable. Sell failed recombinations.

⚔️ Delirium (Cluster Jewels)

  • Medium 4-passive: Mob Mentality + Warning Call
  • Medium 4-passive: Mob Mentality + Lead By Example
  • Small 2-passive: Lead By Example
  • Delirium Orbs (Threaded) for map juice

Simulacrum splinters → Simulacrum (Wave 20-30). Or buy clusters from Simulacrum farmers.

🗺️ Atlas Strategy (3.30 Threads)

  • Shaped Regions: Complete objectives for free Threads
  • Abyss/Breach/Ritual Threads for map juice
  • Frayed Threads (random Thread or vendor)
  • Kirac Missions for Essence/Harvest
  • Blighted Maps (fallback farm - no gear needed)

3.30: Scarabs removed → Threads. Push 8 Threads for max rewards. Blighted Maps = no-gear fallback.

💰 Sell List (Don't Hoard)

  • Excess Essences (keep only Contempt/Dread/Loathing/Zeal/Torment/Horror)
  • Failed Recombinations (failed prefixes/suffixes → vendor or sell cheap)
  • Low-tier Harvest crafts (keep only Phys Reduct, Aug Phys, Aug Life, Aug Armour, Aug Speed)
  • Excess Cluster Jewels (wrong notables)
  • Low-quality Vaal Axes (<27% quality)
  • Excess Elders Exalts / Tempering / Warlord Exalts (sell high, buy when needed)
  • Frayed Threads (vendor for random Thread or sell)

Liquidity > Hoarding. Sell at peak demand (week 1-2). Buy back cheaper later.
